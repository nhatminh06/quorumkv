Filesystem note: the workspace filesystem reported very small fsync syscall
durations (microseconds in aggregate), so these runs primarily expose encoding,
copying, allocation, and physical-byte scaling. They do not represent durable
media latency; adding slower storage would increase the cost of amplification.

Decision: Phase B is justified. Append latency, bytes written, allocation, and
follower persistence all scale with retained history rather than new data.
